# AI Quiz

Three 10-question quizzes: AI pixel art vs real pixel art, AI writing vs real writing, and AI poems vs real poems. Each question has exactly one AI piece; the rest are made by people. Plain HTML/CSS/JS.

Credits for every real piece are in `credits.json` and on the site's credits page. Real pixel art comes from OpenGameArt.org artists (CC0, CC BY 3.0, OGA-BY, CC BY-SA), each credited by name. Real passages and poems are public-domain texts from Project Gutenberg. AI pixel art was generated and then converted in code only; AI passages and poems were written by AI for this quiz.
